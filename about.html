Our Background

The Journey of Royal Computers

Royal Computers was founded with a singular vision: to bring a premium, retail-luxury shopping and service experience to the technology domain. We realized that while tech is highly advanced, local computer sales and repair servicing often lacks professional execution, trust, and premium care.

Over the past decade, we have built a reputation as the most trusted computer sales and service center, serving individual power users, small businesses, academic institutions, and corporate enterprises. We specialize in providing custom-configured systems, hardware component diagnostics, high-security surveillance, and network installations.

Our Milestones

Our Timeline

2016
Foundation

Royal Computers opens its first luxury retail and service lounge, providing diagnostic services and desktop repairs with a small team of two engineers.

2019
Custom PC Lab Launch

We launched our customized PC assembly wing, building bespoke systems for gaming and media creation while expanding our portfolio to include high-speed networking.

Enterprise CCTV & AMC Division

Expansion into industrial surveillance camera systems and corporate annual maintenance contracts (AMCs). Serviced over 3,000 corporate workstations and security grids.

Consolidated our operations into a premium digital experience center. Partnered with major global hardware brands as an authorized sales and service dealer.
